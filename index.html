01 / The idea

Social should feel social again.

Less performance. More personality. The experience keeps the people and interests you choose close, while giving every conversation enough room to breathe.

Your people, within reach

Circles keep close friends, family, teammates, and collaborators easy to find.

Calm by design

Clear choices and thoughtful pacing put you in charge of your attention.

Conversation over counts

Posts are built to invite a response, not turn connection into a scoreboard.
